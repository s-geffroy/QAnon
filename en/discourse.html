Exploratory stylometry: one author or several?

In 2022, two independent teams (OrphAnalytics; Florian Cafiero and Jean-Baptiste Camps) concluded through stylometry that the early drops were written by Paul Furber and later ones by Ron Watkins, with a switch around late 2017 – early 2018. A simple, transparent version of this kind of analysis is reproduced here, without reference texts from candidate authors.

The sharpest split falls on Nov 25, 2017. A second split, in the later part, appears around Jul 29, 2019. This date coincides with the first drop on 8ch /pol (Nov 25, 2017), so a change of author and a change of platform cannot be told apart here.

The five best split dates (between/within ratio) — differences this small show the exact date is fragile: Nov 25, 2017 (1.127), Dec 9, 2017 (1.125), Dec 19, 2017 (1.118), Nov 20, 2017 (1.117), Nov 11, 2017 (1.115)

Method

Consecutive drops are grouped into 89 chunks of about 1,500 words, and each chunk is described by the frequencies of the 150 most frequent words (mostly function words). Burrows' Delta measures the distance between chunks, and we look for the split date that best separates them (between-group / within-group distance ratio).

The limits come from very short texts, shifting topics, quotes and link lists. A change in style may also reflect a change of platform, topic or format. This analysis does not identify any author.
