Q and world news

Q claimed to reveal what the media hide. Here, the actors and countries it names are compared with the news volume GDELT devotes to them — a global database of events extracted from the press (181,749,399 events over 1,219 days).

Method

Over-representation = actor's share of Q's mentions ÷ its share of GDELT volume, over the same set of actors. For the dynamics, we use the correlation of weekly changes (differences of log(1+n)), lagged −4 to +4 weeks (positive: news precedes Q), and Granger tests both ways (minimum p-value over 1 and 2 weeks, uncorrected for multiple comparisons).
