Was Q right?

  • In the horoscope test (300 drops, blind), the model recognises the real news period in 75% of decided cases for the 7 days before the drop, and in 68% for the 30 days after. Yet 6% of randomly drawn periods still get a score of at least 5 out of 10.
  • 17 prediction subjects (same event, same actor) are announced in at least three different months (74 predictions). In 7 of them the announcement returns after a checked failure.

Was Q speaking for Trump?

  • A Trump tweet follows a drop within 5 minutes in 4.2% of cases, versus 4.0% expected by chance (difference not significant, p = 0.227).
  • The event that most raised curiosity about QAnon on Wikipedia was First widely covered "Q" signs at a Trump rally in Tampa (views ×13.3 the following week).

Who wrote, and how?

  • Stylometry places the sharpest change in style around Nov 25, 2017, when Q moved to 8chan. This is consistent with the 2022 analyses of a change of author, but cannot be told apart from a change of platform.
  • On average 1.4 question marks per drop. The rhetorical question is the signature of Q's style.
  • Q posts in bursts. 26% of drops follow the previous one within 5 minutes (burstiness 0.91), grouped into 1,743 sessions (Statistics page).
  • The longest silence lasted 563 days, from Dec 8, 2020 to Jun 24, 2022.

What did Q talk about?

  • The most-named actors are Donald Trump (386), Mainstream media (241), Barack Obama (166), FBI (153), Hillary Clinton (131), by number of drops.
  • Compared with world news volume (GDELT), the actors Q most over-represents are George Soros (×15.8), Hillary Clinton (×8.0), Democratic Party (×3.1).
  • Outside social media, the most linked news outlet is foxnews.com (149 drops), far ahead of thehill.com (41).

Where did Q come from?

  • On /pol/, the board where Q was born, 16 of the 55 dated formulas appear first in Q's posts, and 16 were already circulating before. Q threads make up 6.9% of the board's posts in November 2017, then almost nothing.
